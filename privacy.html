Privacy Policy

Lilly's Crayon Adventures (Android app com.marsanastudio.lilly)
Effective date: 1 October 2026

In short: the game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no chat, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not use the internet to play.

Who we are

Marsana Studio is an independent developer in Kosovo. You can reach us at marsanastudio@gmail.com.

What the game keeps, and where

  • Game progress (finished levels, hearts, stars, gems, clothes and settings) is saved only on the device, inside the app's own storage. We cannot see it.
  • If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this game progress in the device's backup to the user's Google account. That backup is handled by Google, is private to the user, and we have no access to it.
  • Uninstalling the app, or choosing For grown-ups → Reset all progress, deletes the saved progress from the device.

What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, photos, voice, location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising IDs, usage statistics or crash reports. The app asks for no special permissions.

Read-aloud

The "Read to me" button uses the text-to-speech voice that is already installed on the device. The text is spoken by the device itself; nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Some levels may in future be unlocked with a single in-app purchase. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive payment details; Google Play only tells the app whether the full game has been bought, so that it can be unlocked again after reinstalling. Purchases are placed behind a simple grown-ups check.

Children

The game is made for children aged 6 and up. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect any from children. The game contains no ads, no links that lead out of the app, and no way to talk to other people.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect in the app.

Questions

Parents and guardians can write to marsanastudio@gmail.com with any question about this policy.

Çfarë ruan loja dhe ku

Blerjet janë pas një kontrolli të thjeshtë për të rriturit.
