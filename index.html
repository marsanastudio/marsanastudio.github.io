Small games, drawn by hand

Marsana Studio is a family studio. Our games start as real drawings and paper dolls made by our kids, and turn into gentle 3D worlds for children to explore.

Lilly's Crayon Adventures

Ages 4+No adsWorks offline

Lilly is a paper doll who explores 50 hand-drawn maps: every room of her home, a park, a castle, Candy Land, a fun fair with real rides, a dino valley and many more. Kids learn letters, numbers, animals, nature, space, fashion, architecture, computers and AI through short fact cards that can be read out loud, play small quizzes, and try jumping, memory, racing and maze challenges.

No accounts, no chat, no personal data collected. Read our privacy policy.
